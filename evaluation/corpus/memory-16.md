# Handover expiry

The architecture proposal treats handover as a content kind, not a visibility scope. Handovers are personal by default, may be explicitly shared with an authorized project, and expire after seven days by default. Saving unfinished work or hypotheses does not make them accepted knowledge. Arbitrary person-to-person sharing is outside the first pilot.
